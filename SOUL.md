# PAPYRUS — Architectura Vitae (The Soul Manifesto)

## 1. Philosophical Foundations: The Sovereign Curriculum

PAPYRUS exists on the conviction that an individual's career narrative is personal, sensitive, and private property. A resume is not an engagement metric or training data for ad networks; it is a document of human achievement, dignity, and intellectual labor.

### The Five Inviolable Virtues

1. **Absolute Privacy & Zero Intermediaries**
   - 100% offline-first and client-side sovereign.
   - Profile data, employment history, and contact details reside strictly in local storage.
   - All compilation—whether TeX, vector PDF, high-DPI rasterization, or ATS analysis—executes inside the user's browser runtime.

2. **Typographic Dignity & Editorial Truth**
   - Inspired by classical Latin typography, fine book printing, and academic publishing.
   - Clean, balanced proportions, warm neutral surfaces, and legible hierarchy.
   - Rejection of deceptive ATS hacks (white text, invisible keyword stuffing, robotic templates). True ATS parsing thrives on structural semantic clarity.

3. **The Discipline of Simplicity (Ponytail Engineering)**
   - Lean architecture built upon `@ruivalente99/bibliotheca` primitives.
   - Standard browser APIs before heavy dependencies.
   - Clean code, deterministic builds, and minimal abstractions.

4. **Longevity & Portability**
   - Open standards: JSON Resume, LaTeX (.tex), PDF/UA-1, and structured JSON.
   - The user must never be locked in. Any document created in Papyrus can be exported to compilable TeX or raw data at any moment.

5. **Sobriety and Technical Elegance**
   - Zero emojis across code, git logs, and professional document outputs.
   - Understated, confident aesthetics with the 7 signature palettes from Bibliotheca.
