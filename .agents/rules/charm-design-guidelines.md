# TypeUI · Charm Design System Guidelines

When building or updating UI components, follow the Charm design system principles:

1. **Geometry & Radii**:
   - Primary interactive controls, search fields, segmented tabs, and badges use pill radius (`rounded-full`).
   - Cards and structural sections use generous rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl` ~ 16px–24px).
2. **Color Palette & Contrast**:
   - Light theme uses warm neutral foundations: warm page background (`#f7f7f5`), card soft (`#fbfaf9`), and warm band tone (`#f1f2ea`).
   - Text hierarchy: headings in deep charcoal (`#1c1917`), body in mid-stone (`#57534e`), secondary hints in subtle stone (`#79716b`).
   - Dark theme uses matching warm deep neutrals (`bg-stone-900`, `border-stone-800`, text `stone-100`).
3. **Typography**:
   - Headings: Display bold with tight tracking (`tracking-tight`).
   - Eyebrows & Tags: Monospace uppercase with tracking (`tracking-wider`, `text-xs`).
4. **Bento & Feature Cards**:
   - Clean border separators, tinted icon background containers, and soft hover transitions.
